# Modal Strength And Limits

Use this reference to calibrate Discussion language.

## Modal Strength

From cautious to strong:

- may, might, could
- appears to, seems to
- suggests, is consistent with
- supports
- indicates
- demonstrates
- establishes

Use strong verbs only when evidence is direct, controlled, and sufficiently broad.

## When To Soften

Soften claims when:

- Sample, dataset, or benchmark scope is narrow.
- The study lacks ablation or control.
- Results are indirect.
- Baseline comparison is incomplete.
- Mechanism is plausible but untested.
- The finding is preliminary or exploratory.

## Limitations

Good limitations:

- Affect interpretation.
- Are specific.
- Are connected to results or methods.
- Lead naturally to future work.
- Do not undermine the entire paper unless the evidence truly does.

Avoid:

- Empty limitations such as "more work is needed".
- Limitations unrelated to the study's claims.
- Limitations first appearing only as excuses.

## Future Work

Future work should follow from:

- A limitation.
- A new question raised by results.
- A broader application that requires new evidence.
- A technical extension not yet tested.

For deep learning papers, future work may include:

- More datasets or domains.
- Stronger baseline comparison.
- More seeds or statistical testing.
- Larger-scale training.
- Deployment evaluation.
- Interpretability or mechanism analysis.

## Applications

State applications cautiously unless they were directly tested. Use potential or possible when moving beyond study conditions.
