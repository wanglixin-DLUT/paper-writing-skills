## Result Versus Meaning

Keep distinctions clear:

- Result: measured outcome or observed trend.
- Explanation: possible reason for the result.
- Implication: consequence if the interpretation holds.
- Application: practical use or future deployment.
- Contribution: what the study adds to knowledge or capability.

## Claim Boundary Questions

Ask:

- Does the evidence directly support this claim?
- Is the result isolated by an ablation or control?
- Is the result consistent across datasets, seeds, settings, or samples?
- Could another explanation fit the same result?
- Does a limitation narrow the claim?
- Does the target literature already make a stronger or different claim?

## Literature Mapping

Use citations to perform a function:

- Support agreement with existing work.
- Explain disagreement or contrast.
- Show extension of prior methods or knowledge.
- Position the current contribution.
- Identify remaining uncertainty.

Do not add citations just to decorate the Discussion.

## Deep Learning Claim Patterns

Examples of claim categories:

- Component contribution: ablation supports value of a module.
- Performance contribution: benchmark comparison supports improvement.
- Robustness contribution: cross-domain or perturbation tests support stability.
- Efficiency contribution: latency, memory, or compute results support practicality.
- Analysis contribution: error analysis reveals failure modes or mechanism.

State only the category supported by evidence.
