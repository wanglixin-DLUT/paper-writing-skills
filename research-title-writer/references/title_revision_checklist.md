# Title Revision Checklist

Use this checklist before delivering final title recommendations.

## Fit

- Does the title resemble target-journal titles in length and structure?
- Does it fit the article type?
- Does it use or avoid acronyms according to target convention?
- Is colon use appropriate?

## Accuracy

- Does the title match the paper's actual contribution?
- Does it avoid unsupported scope, causality, deployment, or performance claims?
- Does it align with `content_analysis.md` and `writing_blueprint.md`?
- Are the title expectations fulfilled in Abstract, Results, and Discussion?

## Discoverability

- Are the most important search terms present?
- Are secondary terms moved to keywords where appropriate?
- Does the title attract the intended reader rather than every possible reader?

## Readability

- Is the grammar clear?
- Are noun strings manageable?
- Are prepositions unambiguous?
- Is the title easy to scan quickly?

## Final Output

When providing variants, include:

- Title.
- Best use case.
- Strength.
- Risk or tradeoff.
