# Results Revision Checklist

Use this checklist before delivering a Results draft or revision.

## Structure

- Does the order match aims, hypotheses, experiments, figures, or target-paper convention?
- Does each subsection have a clear result function?
- Are key findings given enough attention?
- Are secondary findings proportionate?
- Is enough method context included for readers who jump to Results?

## Evidence

- Does every number or trend have a source?
- Are figures/tables/logs correctly referenced?
- Are metrics, datasets, splits, and baselines clear?
- Are ablations tied to contribution claims?
- Are missing or suspicious runs flagged?

## Figure And Table Narrative

- Does each visual callout say what to notice?
- Is the central pattern explained in prose?
- Are tables summarized rather than repeated cell by cell?
- Are qualitative examples tied to an interpretive purpose?

## Comparison And Certainty

- Are comparisons fair and clearly scoped?
- Is causal language justified?
- Are implications kept brief or moved to Discussion?
- Is risk-reducing language used where evidence is limited?
- Are result problems acknowledged when they affect interpretation?

## Consistency

- Do Results answer the Introduction gap or aim?
- Do Results follow the Methods protocol?
- Do Results support Abstract and Discussion claims?
- Are terminology and acronyms stable?
- Are claims consistent with `content_analysis.md` and `writing_blueprint.md`?

## Final Checks

- No fabricated numbers.
- No unsupported significance claims.
- No overclaiming from one experiment.
- No unexplained baseline or metric.
- No result list without narrative guidance.
