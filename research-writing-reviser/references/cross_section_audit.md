# Cross-Section Audit

Use this reference for whole-paper or multi-section revision.

## Artifact Priority

Use:

- `content_analysis.md` for facts and evidence boundaries.
- `writing_blueprint.md` for writing strategy and section move maps.
- Current manuscript sections for actual claims.
- Target journal instructions, if available.

## Alignment Checks

Check:

- Title promise is fulfilled by Abstract, Results, and Discussion.
- Abstract contribution matches actual results.
- Introduction gap is answered by Results and Discussion.
- Methods provide enough detail to trust Results.
- Results support Discussion interpretations.
- Discussion claims match Conclusion claims.
- Limitations travel consistently through Discussion, Conclusion, and Abstract if needed.
- Terminology is stable across all sections.

## Common Whole-Paper Problems

- Abstract claims a broader contribution than Results support.
- Introduction gap does not match the actual study.
- Methods omit details needed to interpret Results.
- Results present data without narrative guidance.
- Discussion interprets results not shown.
- Conclusion introduces a new claim.
- Title overpromises scope or application.

## Pre-Submission Questions

- What is the paper's central contribution in one sentence?
- Does every section support that same contribution?
- Where would a reviewer ask for evidence?
- Where might a fast reader misunderstand ownership or scope?
