# Sentence Flow

Use this reference for sentence-level readability.

## Known To New

Put familiar or already mentioned information near the beginning of the sentence. Put new or important information later, where it can become the platform for the next sentence.

## Sentence Openings

Check how sentences begin:

- Repeat a stable term to maintain continuity.
- Use a phrase such as `this result`, `this limitation`, or `this approach` only when the referent is unmistakable.
- Avoid starting too many sentences with author names, citations, or abstract nouns.
- Avoid unclear `-ing` openings when the subject is ambiguous.

## Linking Sentences

Add links when the reader must infer:

- contrast
- cause
- example
- consequence
- restatement
- sequence
- scope change

Use connectors accurately. Do not use therefore unless the previous sentence truly supports a consequence.

## Sentence Length And Density

Watch for:

- Consecutive long sentences.
- Too many `and` links.
- Stacked noun phrases.
- Multiple prepositional phrases.
- Parenthetical overload.
- Delayed main verb.

Fix by:

- Splitting sentences.
- Moving the main verb earlier.
- Turning noun stacks into clauses.
- Repeating key terms rather than packing everything into one phrase.

## Pronoun And Reference Clarity

Check:

- this
- it
- they
- which
- these
- such

If more than one referent is possible, repeat the noun or add a clarifying noun.
