# Conclusion Writing Workflow

Use this workflow after reading upstream artifacts when available.

## Required Inputs

- Target journal conclusion convention.
- Central contribution.
- Key results.
- Main implication or application.
- Limitations or future work, if needed.
- Claim boundaries.

## Upstream Handoff

From `content_analysis.md`, use:

- Contribution candidates.
- Strongest evidence.
- Limitations and missing experiments.
- Claim boundaries.

From `writing_blueprint.md`, use:

- Conclusion move map.
- Expected length and placement.
- Final-message strategy.
- Terminology and certainty preferences.

From Discussion:

- Mature interpretation.
- Literature positioning.
- Limitations and future work.

## Drafting Steps

1. Determine the form.
   - Separate Conclusion or final Discussion paragraph.
   - Approximate length based on target papers.

2. Choose the final message.
   - Identify the contribution the reader should retain.
   - Tie it to evidence.

3. Select moves.
   - Include only moves needed for the target journal and study type.

4. Draft.
   - Start by orienting the reader.
   - Synthesize key achievement and evidence.
   - State implication, application, or knowledge advance.
   - Add limitation or future work if it strengthens accuracy.

5. Revise.
   - Remove new evidence.
   - Remove detailed methods.
   - Remove unsupported broad claims.
   - Clarify ownership.

## Deliverables

For a draft:

- Conclusion text.
- Word count.
- Move map.
- Claim-risk notes.

For a revision:

- Revised Conclusion.
- Main changes.
- Remaining risks or missing inputs.
