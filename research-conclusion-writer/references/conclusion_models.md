# Conclusion Models

Use these moves selectively. Target-paper conventions in `writing_blueprint.md` should guide the final form.

## Generic Conclusion Move Menu

Common moves:

1. What is in the paper.
2. What the paper or study has achieved.
3. Relevant background information.
4. Gap, aim, or need for the study.
5. Method or approach.
6. Key results with evaluative comments.
7. Implications of the results.
8. Potential or actual limitations.
9. Potential or actual applications.
10. How the study advances knowledge.
11. Potential future directions.

Do not include every move by default. A short Conclusion often uses only achievement, key result, contribution, implication, and future direction.

## Separate Conclusion

Use when the target journal has a distinct Conclusion section.

Pattern:

- One sentence restating study scope or aim.
- One to three sentences synthesizing key findings and achievement.
- One sentence on contribution, implication, or application.
- Optional limitation/future work.

## Final Discussion Paragraph

Use when the journal does not have a separate Conclusion.

Pattern:

- Continue from limitations or implications.
- Consolidate the main contribution.
- Close with application, knowledge advance, or future work.

## Short Conclusion

Use when target papers average 100-200 words or less.

Keep:

- Contribution.
- One key result or evidence basis.
- Implication or future direction.

Cut:

- Background.
- Detailed methods.
- Multiple secondary findings.

## Deep Learning Conclusion Variant

Common moves:

- State the proposed model, method, dataset, training strategy, or evaluation contribution.
- Synthesize benchmark or ablation evidence.
- Name the scope where evidence holds.
- State practical or research implication.
- Mention future work tied to data, generalization, compute, deployment, interpretability, or stronger evaluation.

Do not claim generality beyond tested datasets, domains, or protocols.
