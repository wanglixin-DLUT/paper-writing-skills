# Conclusion Revision Checklist

Use this checklist before delivering a Conclusion.

## Fit

- Does the target journal use a separate Conclusion?
- Is the length proportional to target papers?
- Does it avoid duplicating the Abstract?
- Does it fit after the Discussion without abrupt repetition?

## Contribution

- Is the study's achievement or contribution explicit?
- Could any sentence be mistaken for background knowledge rather than this study's finding?
- Is ownership clear through subject, tense, or framing?
- Is the contribution consistent with Abstract and Introduction?

## Evidence And Claims

- Are key results synthesized rather than listed?
- Does every implication follow from Results and Discussion?
- Are limitations or scope boundaries included when needed?
- Are future directions specific and motivated?
- Are applications stated cautiously if not directly tested?

## Coherence

- Does the Conclusion close the narrative opened in the Introduction?
- Does it preserve stable terminology?
- Does it avoid introducing new literature, methods, or results?
- Does the final sentence leave a clear take-home message?

## Final Checks

- No new evidence.
- No unsupported novelty claim.
- No vague "future work" ending.
- No inflated application claim.
- No ambiguous contribution ownership.
