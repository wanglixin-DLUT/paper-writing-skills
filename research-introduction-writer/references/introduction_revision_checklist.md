# Introduction Revision Checklist

Use this checklist before delivering an Introduction draft or revision.

## Structure

- Does the Introduction narrow from field context to the present study?
- Does the opening give the target reader a clear entry point?
- Does the final paragraph clearly describe the present paper?
- Is the order consistent with target-paper conventions in `writing_blueprint.md`?
- Is the Introduction proportionate to the article length and journal style?

## Literature Map

- Does each citation have a clear function?
- Are prior studies grouped and synthesized rather than listed?
- Does the literature map lead toward the user's gap or problem?
- Are major baseline families represented if the paper depends on them?
- Are citations missing for broad claims, prior methods, or limitations?
- Are any references better moved to Discussion?

## Gap And Motivation

- Is the gap specific, current, and defensible?
- Does the gap follow logically from the prior work?
- Is the critique respectful and impersonal?
- Does the user's study actually address the stated gap?
- For deep learning projects, is the gap supported by `content_analysis.md` baselines or experiments?

## Present Study

- Is the aim or study action explicit?
- Is the method preview high-level enough for the Introduction?
- Are result or contribution previews supported by evidence?
- Does the final paragraph avoid becoming an Abstract?
- Are claims aligned with `content_analysis.md` and `writing_blueprint.md`?

## Coherence

- Does each paragraph have one main function?
- Does each paragraph connect to the next?
- Are sentence links explicit where the reader might otherwise infer too much?
- Are stable terms repeated consistently?
- Are pronouns and reference words unambiguous?

## Tense, Voice, And Stance

- Are established facts, specific past studies, and present-study claims clearly distinguished?
- Does active or passive voice make contribution ownership clear?
- Is novelty language proportional to the evidence?
- Are uncertain claims softened where necessary?

## Final Checks

- No fabricated citations.
- No unsupported state-of-the-art claims.
- No method details that belong in Methods.
- No result details that belong in Results unless target papers preview results.
- No literature-dump paragraphs.
- Any missing citations or evidence are flagged for the user.
